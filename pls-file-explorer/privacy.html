PLS File Explorer: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026 · Applies to PLS File Explorer for Android (shown on the phone as "PLS Explorer")

Short version: PLS File Explorer collects nothing and sends nothing to us. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your files stay on your phone, and Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares any data about you. The app uses a network only when you ask it to (Phone to PC, and the speed test in Device status), as described below.

What the app does with your files

PLS File Explorer is a file manager. To show, sort, search, copy, move, zip, unzip, open and clean up your files, it reads and writes the files on your phone's storage and on USB drives you plug in. All of this happens on your phone. Nothing about your files is sent to Pulse Labs or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, bookmarks, favorites, history, hide list, Recycle Bin and the app's PIN (if you set one).
  • The list of computers you paired with Phone to PC, and the keys for them.
  • Thumbnails and the search index the app keeps so pages open quickly.
  • Files you lock with a password are encrypted on your phone; the password is never stored or sent.

The app does not back up its data to any cloud (Android backup is turned off for it). Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, except files you made yourself in your own folders.

Phone to PC (only when you switch it on)

Phone to PC lets you move files between this phone and your own computer on your own Wi-Fi, your phone's hotspot or a USB cable. It is off until you start it. It never uses mobile data and never goes over the internet.

  • Paired PC: you pair a computer once by matching a 6-digit code on both screens. Files then travel encrypted, directly between the phone and that computer. While it is on, the phone announces itself on your local Wi-Fi so your computer can find it.
  • Any PC's browser: a small page you open in a browser on a computer on the same network. It needs the code shown on the phone, shows a notification the whole time it is on, and turns itself off when you close it, tap Stop sharing, or nobody uses it for the idle time you pick. This connection is not encrypted, so use it only on a network you trust.

Files go only to the computer you connect. They never pass through Pulse Labs.

Speed test in Device status (only when you tap it)

Device status can time your connection. Only when you tap the test, the app opens a connection to a public address (1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8), measures how long it takes, and closes it. It sends nothing about you, your phone or your files.

Sharing, Bluetooth and Google Drive

When you share a file, send it by Bluetooth or pick a file from Google Drive, PLS File Explorer hands it to the app or system screen you choose (for example your email app, Android's Bluetooth sender, or the Google Drive app). What happens next is up to that app and its own privacy policy.

Problem reports

If you choose Report a problem, the app fills in an email for you to send from your own email app. Before anything is sent you can see the whole report and untick any part of it: the app version, your phone model and Android version, the screen you were on and the app's settings, and a screenshot. It never includes your PIN, your history, your hidden items, file names or file contents. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use a report only to fix the problem, and you can ask us to delete it.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • All files access: a file manager has to see and manage every file and folder you have, not only pictures or downloads. It is used only to show and work on your files at your request.
  • Install apps: lets you install an app file (.apk) that you pick. Android asks you to confirm every install. The app never downloads or installs anything by itself and never updates itself; updates come from Google Play.
  • Remove apps: lets App manager ask Android to uninstall an app you choose. Android asks you to confirm.
  • Network access: used by Phone to PC, to talk to your own computer on your own network, and by the speed test you tap in Device status (see above). Nothing else connects out.
  • Notifications: progress of long copies, music controls, Phone to PC while it is on, and reminders you switch on yourself.
  • Run in the background: keeps a long copy, move or zip, Phone to PC, or your music going when you switch apps.
  • Fingerprint or face: only to unlock the app if you turn that on. Android checks it; the app never sees your fingerprint or face.
  • Audio settings: the equalizer and bass boost for music the app itself plays.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone calls or text messages, and it does not read your phone's identifiers.

Children

PLS File Explorer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
