Pulse Dark: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026

Short version: Pulse Dark collects nothing and sends nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares any data about you, because Pulse Dark never sends us any.

What Pulse Dark does

Pulse Dark has one job: it shows websites in dark mode. It reads the colors and layout of the page you are looking at, inside your browser, and builds a dark version of it right there. Nothing about the page or about you leaves your browser.

What stays in your browser

  • Your settings: on or off, theme, sliders, schedule, loadouts, and the rules you make for each site (including parts you recolored with the picker).
  • They are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on this computer only. They are not synced and not sent anywhere.
  • If you use "Save settings file" in Options, that file goes only where you save it.

What Pulse Dark downloads, and why

Pulse Dark uploads nothing. The only things it downloads are copies of pictures and style files that the page you are viewing is already using, usually straight from your browser's cache. It does this so it can tell dark icons from light ones and recolor the page's own styles for dark mode.

  • These requests go to the same website that is already showing them to you, not to Pulse Labs.
  • They are plain downloads: no cookies, no referrer, no settings, and nothing about you is added.
  • Only the result (for example "this icon is dark") is used, inside your browser. Nothing is stored or passed on.
  • Pulse Dark never downloads or runs program code. Its fixes for about 2,800 websites are built into the extension and arrive only with an extension update.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Access to all websites: dark mode has to work on every site you visit, so Pulse Dark needs to recolor every page.
  • Storage: saves your own settings in this browser.
  • Scripting: applies dark mode to pages as they load, and to tabs already open when you change a setting.
  • Alarms: runs the optional schedule (for example, dark from sunset to sunrise) and ends a "pause dark on this tab" when the time you picked is up.
  • Context menus: adds Pulse Dark's quick options to the right-click menu.

Children

Pulse Dark collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

Removing Pulse Dark from Chrome removes everything it stored. You can also erase all your settings from Pulse Dark's Options page ("Erase everything").

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
