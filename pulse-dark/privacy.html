Pulse Dark: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 25, 2026

Short version: Pulse Dark doesn't track you. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing about what you browse is ever sent to us. Pulse Labs only receives something when you choose to send it: a page report you send with the green check, or your sign-in when you unlock Pulse Dark Pro. Basic dark mode needs no account at all.

What Pulse Dark does

Pulse Dark shows websites in dark mode. It reads the colors and layout of the page you're looking at, inside your browser, and builds a dark version of it right there. Nothing about the page or about you leaves your browser for that.

What stays in your browser

  • Your settings: on or off, theme, sliders, schedule, loadouts, and the rules you make for each site (including parts you recolored with the picker).
  • They are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on this computer only. They are not synced and not sent anywhere.
  • If you use "Save settings file" in Options, that file goes only where you save it.

What Pulse Dark downloads, and why

  • Pictures and style files the page already uses, usually straight from your browser's cache, so Pulse Dark can tell dark icons from light ones and recolor the page's own styles. These requests go to the website that's already showing them to you, as plain downloads: no cookies, no referrer, nothing about you added.
  • Pulse Labs' list of site fixes, about once a day: small style rules (colors and selectors, never program code) that we write when a site needs help. The request asks only for the list; it carries nothing about you or what you browse. You can turn it off in Options ("Pulse Labs fixes").
  • Pulse Dark never downloads or runs program code. Its built-in fixes for about 2,800 websites arrive with extension updates.

Report this page (only when you send it)

  • When a page doesn't look right, the popup's Report this page button opens a preview of exactly what would be sent: the page address, your note, Pulse Dark's version and its settings for that site, your browser version, and a screenshot of the page as you see it.
  • A screenshot can show private things (a signed-in name, an address, an order). It is shown to you first, and you can untick it. Nothing is sent until you press the green check.
  • Reports go to Pulse Labs' own database (hosted by Supabase in the USA), are used only to fix Pulse Dark, are kept no longer than 180 days, and are never shared or sold.

Pulse Dark Pro (only if you unlock it)

  • Pro is a one-time purchase on pulselabsstudio.com, sold through our reseller Paddle.com, who handles the payment; we never see your card.
  • To unlock Pro on a browser you either paste your Pulse key, or sign in with your email and the code we email you. Pulse Dark then keeps your key in this browser and checks it on this computer.
  • About once a week Pulse Dark asks our server whether your key is still valid (for example after a refund). That request holds your sign-in token and the product name, nothing else.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Access to all websites: dark mode has to work on every site you visit, so Pulse Dark needs to recolor every page.
  • Storage: saves your own settings in this browser.
  • Scripting: applies dark mode to pages as they load, and to tabs already open when you change a setting.
  • Alarms: runs the optional schedule, ends a "pause dark on this tab" when its time is up, and the daily fixes check.
  • Context menus: adds Pulse Dark's quick options to the right-click menu.
  • Screenshot of the page you're on: taken only when you press Report this page, and shown to you before anything is sent.

Children

Pulse Dark is a general-audience tool and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

Removing Pulse Dark from Chrome removes everything it stored. You can also erase all your settings from Pulse Dark's Options page ("Erase everything"). To delete a report you sent or your Pulse account, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
