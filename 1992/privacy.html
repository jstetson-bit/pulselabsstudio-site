1992: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 24, 2026

Short version: 1992 has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares your data. The game plays offline once it has loaded.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: play style, controls, sound, HUD layout, paint job.
  • Your progress: banked salvage, upgrades you bought, reputation and rank, pilot call sign.

These live in your browser's (or the app's) local storage. Clearing the site's data, or uninstalling the app, erases them.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing during play. The web version downloads the game's files from pulselabsstudio.com when you open it, the same way any web page loads.
  • Links you tap (like this page) open in your browser.

Controllers

If you plug in or pair a game controller, the game reads its buttons and sticks on your device to fly the jet. Nothing about the controller is sent anywhere.

Children

1992 collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
