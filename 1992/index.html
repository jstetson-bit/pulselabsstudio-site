How to fly the concept

Two ways to play (Settings, "How you play"; Auto picks for you): Touch (phone upright) is just your finger: put it down and the jet flies and fires; lift it and the guns rest (rested barrels open heavy). Fly close to a big target (a boss section, an engine, the bomber) and hold there: beeps, then the long tone, and two locked missiles go (3x damage each), so break away. Double-tap where you're flying to barrel roll (untouchable for the roll; 20 s recharge; with two charges, double-tap twice). On the right: BOMB shows your charges (3 to start), and above it a slot for whatever you're holding (wingman calls today): tap to use. No gun heat in Touch. The HUD lives in the steel banner across the top: systems and roll on the left, score (your hazard pay) and the mission clock in the middle, salvage on the right. Salvage: every kill drops scrap; fly through the wreckage to grab it (a short tractor pulls in pieces you nearly touch, so you have to go to it). Big kills drop more, and a big chunk is worth 5. Salvage banks in the hangar and buys upgrades (the shop opens in a later build). The flight board (hangar, FLIGHT BOARD · UPGRADES): spend banked salvage. A blueprint of your jet slides to the part you pick. Flight controls (response +25/30/35%), engines (burner speed and recharge), fuel tanks (burner lasts longer), guns (+25/50/75% damage), missiles (damage and more rails), pods (stronger pills; at Mk 2 a gun pod jumps two levels), salvage tractor (more reach), and paint jobs. Three marks each; the green check buys the next one. The contract: PLAY opens it first. Two terms: SALVAGE RIGHTS (your share of the wreckage you grab; get shot down and you lose the field, so no salvage) and HAZARD PAY (a share of your score, paid in salvage either way). You get 3 asks: ask for +10% on either; the client says yes or no, and how far they will move is hidden: it rises with your reputation and wobbles every contract, so feel for the number. TRADE swaps 10% pay for 10% salvage, always accepted. The green check signs and flies. Reputation: +2 for a stage clear, +1 more with no hits, -1 if you punch out. Ranks: Green, Regular (3), Veteran (8), Elite (15), Ace (25). Menu: the ghost ☰ in the top right (Start on a controller) pauses and holds how-to-play, invert colors, edit HUD and quit; it fades after 10 seconds untouched and wakes when you touch it; move it with EDIT HUD. Pad is a controller on the screen: the 8-way hat on the left flies, the six buttons on the right do the rest (pick it any time; made for big screens). Pilot (sideways, PC, or a controller) is the full pad below; its left thumb is your choice in Settings (finger, hat or stick).

Controls (an Xbox-style pad, same on touch and a real controller): X = GUN, Y = BOMB, B = MISSILE, A = call a WINGMAN, LB = ROLL, RB = BURNER, LT = VIEW, right trigger also fires. GUN (hold to fire; GUN TEMP in the top-left fills as the barrels heat up, and hot barrels fire slower, so shoot in bursts; rest it 2 seconds and it glows, and your next burst opens heavy; any power-up pod snaps the barrels cold and readies your roll) is the big button on the left of the diamond, BOMB on top, MSL on the right (tap to fire; hold to bring up the lock reticle, let go to fire the locked missile, 3x damage), WING (call a wingman) at the bottom; BURN and ROLL sit above. ROLL makes you untouchable for a moment, then takes 20 seconds to recharge, so save it. Left thumb flies: drag anywhere, or turn on the Top hat (an 8-way pad) in the settings. LT changes the view, ☰ pauses. Left thumb flies: drag anywhere (Finger), an 8-way pad (Top hat), or a thumbstick that appears wherever you put your thumb on the left half (Thumbstick); pick it in the settings. The launch counts itself down.

Power-ups (armored pods about a quarter the size of your jet; they drift and bounce off the screen edges twice, so chase them down before they get away; what they give lasts the whole stage): red crackling P = gun power (bigger, wider, redder guns, three levels); M with six missiles under it = +6 missiles, and Sidewinders launch on their own while you hold GUN (every 2 s, then 1 s, then 0.6 s); white R = an extra roll (your ROLL holds two); cyan O = a satellite drone that circles you, shoots with you, blocks rounds and grinds anything it touches (up to two); blue S = speed; green + = life; yellow B = a bomb (BOMB button, or N): it drops, arms for 2 seconds, then a flash, a shock ring and a mushroom cloud take out everything on screen but you. BURNER really speeds you up: chase down the one that got past you. The Tu-16 bomber at the end stays until you bring it down.

Your plane's stats count in the stage: Speed = how fast you move around the screen (the Hornet is the quickest), Armor = how much a hit hurts, Guns = how hard your rounds hit. Blue S capsule = more speed (three levels).

Stage controls: VIEW and LAUNCH low on the left; on the right, MSL (small circle) above GUN, BURNER under it. On the F-14 the burner sweeps the wings back; they open again after.

Play (green check, or PLAY · STAGE 1): launch off the carrier, hit BURNER to climb, then fight. Your left thumb flies the jet: drag anywhere and the jet follows, offset so your thumb never covers it (keys: arrows or WASD). Right thumb: hold GUN for two streams of fire (Space), tap MSL for a missile (M or Enter): 6 Sidewinders, 3 on each wing, one per tap, they chase the nearest MiG ahead. BURNER (B): faster for 3 seconds. MiGs come from the front and from behind: the red marks at the bottom edge and "six o'clock" warning mean bandits behind you; your guns only fire forward, so dodge, then shoot them as they pass. On the catapult you have 10 seconds: VIEW (or V) looks from the seat forward, left and right; LAUNCH (or L) fires the catapult early. VIEW works in flight too. ↶ goes back to the hangar. Paint (Ready Room): factory, OD green, Navy blue, desert tan or night; the markings stay. More paints get earned in missions later. Missile drill: the original practice run (one missile chasing you: burner, flares, roll, air brake, guns). Flares never run out; neither does the gun.

Sound credits: recordings by dklon, Michel Baradari, and AntumDeluge (from jakobthiesen), CC-BY 3.0; plus CC0 sounds by theMinesAreShakin and rubberduck (opengameart.org).

Move: drag anywhere, or arrow keys / WASD.

Music: Settings → Music source. Game music, your own songs (music files on this device; the list stays in the game), your music app (start Spotify, Amazon Music or any player, and the game plays its sounds over it), or off.

View: the View button (or V) steps through top-down, cockpit, left and right. You can fly the whole fight from the cockpit: the other jets, bullets and the fortress are all there in 3D around you. Top-down is easier for dodging; the seat is the thrill.

Climb: when the carrier orders it, tap Climb (or C). If you wait, the jet climbs on its own.

Burner (B): 3 seconds of afterburner. Faster climb, outruns a missile, burns fuel fast.

Flares (F): when a missile is locked on, drop them when it's close (inside 1 mile). Too early and it ignores them. Or burn hard long enough and its motor runs out first.

Air brake (X): slows you hard. Roll as the missile closes, then hit the air brake: it flies under you and ends up in front, and you get 5 seconds to gun it down for bonus pay. Guns: hold GUN (or Space) to fire; let go to stop. Fighters carry the M61 Vulcan (100 rounds a second), the A-10 the GAU-8 Avenger (65 a second, with its famous BRRRT echo), the AC-130 a 40 mm Bofors (single heavy shots). The SR-71, B-2, E-2C and C-5 carry no gun.

Missile (M): six Sidewinders, three on each wing. Once the enemy missile is in front of you (air brake or a perfect roll puts it there), fire one at it: it homes in. +300.

Roll (R): a barrel roll. Roll as the missile closes (inside about 0.4 NM) and it overshoots, and the camera cuts to your visor in slow motion as it passes under you.

Start your playlist in your music app, then come back: the game plays its engines, guns and explosions over your music and leaves the music alone.

The bar at the top is the mission: launch, angels 16, clear. The clock beats par for a better medal.
