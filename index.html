About

Pulse Labs Studio is the software and media studio of Pulse National LLC (Florida, USA). We build design-led Android apps, games and audiobooks. Our rule is simple: give people the service and the money will follow — core function free, extras for one fair one-time price, no subscriptions, no ads, no currency games.

First release

EVA Clock

A clock and info panel that lives on a tablet in a truck cab: giant readable digits, date, weather for where you've been and where you are, and a mail counter — every control is the thing itself, tapped directly, nothing buried in menus. Built by a working trucker who refused to pay a yearly toll for a clock.

Core free forever. Customization is a one-time unlock — no subscription, no ads.

Coming soon to Google Play.
