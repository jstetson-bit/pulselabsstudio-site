PLS Faceplate

Android car stereo

A better home screen for your Android car stereo. Those big vertical stereos come with a flashy stock theme that shows km/h, cuts off its own labels and can't be rearranged. PLS Faceplate takes over the home screen and fixes all of it, and it stays light so the stereo stays fast.

In development. First built for the 9.7-inch Hummer H2 stereo (7862 chip).

Your units, your layout

Speed in mph or km/h. Move and size every panel: speed, weather, music, compass, gauges. Your layout comes back after every key-off.

Reads your truck

Connects to a Bluetooth OBD adapter for real speed, RPM, temperatures, volts and trouble codes, including older GM trucks that use J1850 VPW. If an adapter can't talk to your truck, it says so in plain words.

Keeps what works

One tap opens the stereo's own radio, Bluetooth phone, 360 camera and CarPlay / Android Auto. The stock home screen stays one tap away.

No strings

No ads, no account, no tracking. Internet only for the weather and checking for updates.
