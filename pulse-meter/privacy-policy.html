Pulse Meter: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs · Effective November 14, 2026

Short version: Pulse Meter does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your data. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

What Pulse Meter reads

On the one website it is built for (the site listed under "Access" in its store listing) and nowhere else, Pulse Meter reads your own usage figures: the 5-hour session and weekly percentages and their reset times. These are the same figures that site's own Settings → Usage page shows you. It reads them using the session you're already signed in with on that site. It never sees or stores your password, and it doesn't read your conversations or messages.

What stays on your device

  • Your Pulse Meter settings (look, position, sizes, colors, fonts) and the most recent usage reading, kept in your browser's extension storage.
  • Any font files you choose to add, kept in the same local storage.
  • If you allow it, your browser's list of installed font names, used only to fill the font picker.
  • A crash log of the last 50 errors, which only you can see. It's copied to your clipboard only when you tap "Copy crash log".

What we send anywhere

Nothing. Pulse Meter has no servers, analytics, ads, or tracking, and it never sends your data to Pulse Labs or anyone else.

Permissions

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: keep your settings and any font files you add on your device.
  • scripting / activeTab: start the meter in open tabs of that one site and let the toolbar popup control it.
  • Access to that one site: the meter only runs there.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Pulse Meter removes everything it stored. You can also clear the crash log or use "Reset to default" any time.

Independence

Pulse Meter is an independent tool by Pulse Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the operator of the site it works on.
