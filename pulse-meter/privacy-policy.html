Pulse Meter: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 15, 2026

Short version: Pulse Meter collects nothing, stores everything only inside your own browser, and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking.

What Pulse Meter does

Pulse Meter is a Chrome extension that shows your Claude.ai session and weekly usage as bars in the extension popup. To do that it reads the usage figures shown on claude.ai while you are signed in there, and only while you are on claude.ai.

What stays in your browser

  • Your display preferences for the extension (look, colors, options you pick).
  • The most recent usage figures it read from claude.ai, so the popup can show them instantly.

All of this is kept in Chrome's own extension storage on your device. It is never transmitted to Pulse Labs or to any third party.

What Pulse Meter never does

  • It never collects, receives, stores, sells or shares personal information.
  • It never reads any site other than claude.ai.
  • It never runs analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Permissions, in plain words

  • storage / unlimitedStorage — keep your preferences and the last usage figures in your browser.
  • scripting / activeTab — read the usage numbers on claude.ai when you open the popup or the usage page.
  • Host permission for claude.ai only — the extension can only run on claude.ai.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
