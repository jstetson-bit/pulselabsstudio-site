PlsMark: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026 · Windows and Android

Short version: PlsMark takes your screenshots and recordings and keeps them on your own computer or phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cloud. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares your pictures, recordings or anything else.

What stays on your device

  • Your screenshots, recordings, GIFs and .pmark projects, saved only where you choose (and copied to your clipboard when you finish one).
  • Your settings, hotkeys and recent colors.
  • A crash log of recent errors. Only you can see it; it is sent only if you choose to email it to us yourself.

What PlsMark uses, and why

  • The screen: to take the screenshot or recording you start. Nothing is captured until you ask.
  • The Print Screen key and your other hotkeys: PlsMark listens for the keys you set so a snip starts when you press them. It does not record or keep any other keystrokes.
  • Microphone and webcam (only if you turn them on for a recording): to add your voice or a camera bubble to that recording.
  • Text grab: reading text out of a picture uses Windows' own built-in text recognition, on your PC.
  • Start with Windows: so Print Screen works right after you sign in. Switch it off in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.
  • Internet: PlsMark doesn't need it. Links in the app open in your browser only when you click them.

Children

PlsMark is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Removing your data

Uninstalling PlsMark removes the app and its settings. Pictures and recordings you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
