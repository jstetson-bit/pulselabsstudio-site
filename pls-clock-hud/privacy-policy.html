PLS Clock HUD: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026

Short version: PLS Clock HUD has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares your data. Almost everything happens on your device. The only things that leave it are the few look-ups listed below, sent straight to free public services, and only when you have that item on screen.

What stays on your device

  • Your layout, colors, fonts, themes and every setting.
  • Your saved weather places, reminders, links and trip, leg and lifetime mileage.
  • Today's driving time and the recent elevation trace used for the hills display.
  • A crash log of recent errors. Only you can see it, and it is sent only if you tap to email it to us yourself.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Weather (only with a weather card on screen): your approximate location, or the place you typed, goes to Open-Meteo to get the forecast and to look up place names.
  • Speed limit sign (only while the sign is on screen): your current position goes to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API to find the posted limit on the road you're on, about once every 300 m.
  • Check for updates / Privacy policy / links you add: these open in your browser or Google Play only when you tap them.

These services receive the request the same way any website would (your IP address and the position or place in the request). Pulse Labs is not in the middle and keeps nothing. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Location: speed, direction, mileage, elevation, the speed limit sign and the LIVE weather card. Used only while the app (or the floating DRIVE display you start) is showing them. It is never sent to Pulse Labs.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps the floating DRIVE display updating over other apps, with a notification showing while it runs. Stop it from that notification any time.
  • Display over other apps: the floating DRIVE display.
  • Notification access (optional, you turn it on): counts unread mail and messages for the status icons and shows what's playing for the media item. Notification content is read on the device only and never stored or sent.
  • Phone state: shows 5G / LTE and your carrier's signal bars. It does not read your phone number, calls or contacts.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state: the online/offline, Wi-Fi and Wi-Fi version icons. Fine location is required by Android to show your Wi-Fi network's name.
  • Alarms, full-screen alerts, notifications, vibrate, start at boot: your reminders ring on time, even after a restart.
  • Internet: only the look-ups listed above.

Children

PLS Clock HUD is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also reset trips, legs, the drive timer and the crash log from inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
