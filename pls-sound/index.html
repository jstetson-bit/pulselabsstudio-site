PLS Sound

Windows PC

Record what your PC plays, straight to MP3. A video, a browser tab, a game: press Record and PLS Sound captures it exactly as you hear it, with no microphone and no room noise. Or record your microphone. It saves a real MP3, up to 320 kbps.

In testing on Windows 11. The player joins it next; a phone version comes later.

What's playing

Anything your PC plays, recorded from the sound itself. Turn the room's noise up all you like.

Real MP3

128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps, made by Windows' own encoder. Plays everywhere.

Any sound card

96 and 192 kHz cards and surround setups work too.

Only when you say

Records only while you press Record. Offline, no account, light on your PC.

Fair use

Please record only what you're allowed to. Many sites, YouTube included, don't permit copying their sound except for your own personal use.
