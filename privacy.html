Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio (Pulse National LLC). Applies to this website and to every app and extension we publish, unless an app's own page says more. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

The short version

Our apps don't track you. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking of any kind, and nothing about you is ever sold. We only receive something when you choose to send it: when you buy, when you sign in to unlock what you bought, or when you send us a report.

What our apps and extensions do on your device

  • Your settings, themes and pictures stay on your device.
  • Each app's own page lists anything it fetches from the internet and why (for example weather for a clock, or the site style files Pulse Dark reads to darken a page). Those requests carry nothing about you.

EVA Clock / PLS Clock HUD (Android): what it does on your device

  • Mail count. The app counts your unread mail on the device by counting mail notifications through Android's notification-listener service. It never opens your mail account, never reads message content, and the count never leaves your device.
  • Weather. To show weather where you are and where you've stopped, the app sends your approximate location to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a third-party weather service, and displays what comes back. That request is between your device and the weather service; we receive nothing from it.
  • Fuel prices. The fuel widget fetches published prices directly from Pilot Company (pilotcompany.com). No personal information is included in that request.
  • Photos and themes. Background pictures you pick stay on your device.

EVA Clock / PLS Clock HUD permissions, in plain words

  • Location (approximate and precise) — so the weather and "where you are" readouts can work.
  • Notification access — only to count mail notifications for the mail counter, on-device.
  • Phone state — to show your cellular signal readout.
  • Bluetooth — to show the Bluetooth status icon.
  • Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state — to fetch weather and fuel prices and show connection status.

When you buy

  • Payments are handled by our reseller Paddle.com (Merchant of Record). Paddle collects your payment details, billing country and email to take the payment and charge tax; we never see your card number. See Paddle's privacy notice.
  • Paddle tells us what was bought, when, the price, and the email address; we keep that as your order and your license.

Your Pulse account

  • An account is your email address (confirmed with a code or link we email you). We store the email, your orders and your Pulse keys. That's all.
  • When an app checks your purchase (for example Pulse Dark Pro, about once a week), it sends your sign-in token and the product name, and gets back your key. It sends nothing about what you browse or do.
  • Accounts and orders are stored with Supabase (our database host, servers in the USA). Emails are sent through our mail provider.

Reports you send ("Report this page", "Report a problem")

  • Nothing is ever sent on its own. You press Report, you see exactly what will be sent, and you press the green check.
  • A Pulse Dark page report holds: the page address, your note, the extension's version and settings for that site, your browser version, and, only if you leave it ticked, a screenshot of the page as you see it. A screenshot can show private things (a signed-in name, an address, an order): untick it if you don't want it sent.
  • Reports are used only to fix our products, kept no longer than 180 days, and never shared or sold.

What we never do

  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics, no trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No subscriptions. Paid upgrades are one-time purchases.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, at any time: write to us from the email on your account.

Children

Our products are general-audience tools and are not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect anything from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date, and the change is stated plainly at the top.
