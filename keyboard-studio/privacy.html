Keyboard Studio: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026

Short version: What you type never leaves your phone. Keyboard Studio has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your keyboard look: layout, key shapes, colors, fonts, pictures and every setting, plus fonts and key pictures you import.
  • Words the keyboard learns so its suggestions fit you (your own words and the short word patterns you use most). This is turned off in password, PIN, email-address and other private boxes. You can clear all of it at any time with "Forget the words I taught it" in the Advanced editor.
  • Your clipboard list (things you copied), so you can paste them again from the keyboard. You can turn it off, or clear it with "Clear clipboard history".
  • Screenshots and camera pictures you take with the keyboard's own keys, saved in your Pictures/KeyboardStudio folder.
  • A crash log of recent errors. Only you can see it. It leaves the phone only if you tap REPORT and choose to send it yourself, for example by email.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, by Keyboard Studio. The app has no internet access. Links you open from the keyboard open in your own browser.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Microphone (only when you press the mic key): voice typing. The sound goes to your phone's own speech service (the one built into Android), which turns it into words. Keyboard Studio does not record, save or send any sound. Your phone's speech service follows its own maker's privacy policy.
  • Screen capture (only after you say yes to Android's prompt): the screenshot key. A notification shows while it is on. Pictures are saved only on your phone.
  • Vibrate: the small buzz when you press a key.

Children

Keyboard Studio is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, except pictures you chose to save to your Pictures folder. You can also use RESET to go back to the starting look, and "Forget the words I taught it" to clear learned words.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
