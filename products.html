How the store works

Products lists every Pulse Labs app. Buying here always costs the same or less than Google Play, and bundles cost less again.

Your account: make one with your email and a password. We send you an email; tap the link in it and your account is verified. Only verified accounts can check out, so every purchase is tied to you.

Paying: the payment window comes from Paddle, our reseller. Cards and PayPal work there, and Paddle keeps any saved payment details. Your card number never reaches us.

After you buy: your Pulse key appears under Account, My products. Open the app, tap the ghost "i", then Unlock, and paste the key. It works offline, forever, on the copy from our site or from Google Play.
