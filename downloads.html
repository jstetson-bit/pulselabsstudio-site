How Downloads works

Each card is one app. Tap it and it jumps open to the full page: screenshots, what it does, what changed, and the download buttons.

Phone apps come as an APK. PC apps come as an exe or zip. Each file shows its size and a fingerprint (SHA-256) so you can check it arrived untouched.

Use the chips at the top to show only Android or only PC. The ◐ button flips the colors.
