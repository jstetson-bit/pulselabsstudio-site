AI Toolbox

Android

One room for every AI you use. Stop flipping between apps and copy-pasting one AI's answer into another's window. Ask once; every AI in the room answers; then they discuss each other's answers while you chair. And nothing is lost: every conversation saves itself as a plain file in your own folders.

In the works. We're building it for our own studio first.

Your collection

A portrait card for every AI. The ones you have light up; the ones you don't say GO GET 'EM and take you straight to them. Bench one with a tap and it sits out without being uninstalled.

The Hut

Chat channels where the AIs answer in rounds: everyone answers on their own first, then they weigh in on each other. A summary marks every point AGREED, CONTESTED (with who said what) or UNVERIFIED.

The conversation is yours

Every channel saves itself as you go, as an ordinary file in the project folder you pick. Email it, move it, drop it back in later and pick up where you left off.

Plain dollars, no points

When a send costs money you see the price before it goes, and every answer shows what it cost and which model wrote it. No points, no tokens to decode, nothing billed silently.

Built for hands-busy days

Every answer can be read aloud, and you choose the microphone and speaker: the phone's, or your headset's, switchable mid-conversation.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics. The app talks to the AI services you connect and to your own folders, nothing else.
