Pulse Meds: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026 · Applies to Pulse Meds from Google Play

Short version: Pulse Meds from Google Play has no internet permission, so Android does not let it go online. It collects nothing and sends nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud copy, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares your medications, your health records or anything about how you use the app.

Reminders only, not medical advice. Pulse Meds is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device. Always follow your pharmacy label and your doctor.

What Pulse Meds does

It reminds you when a dose is due, records what you took or skipped when you confirm it, keeps track of how many you have left, and holds your own health notes, appointments and emergency contacts, all on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your medications and schedules: names, strengths, doses, reminder times, supply counts and notes.
  • Your history: doses taken, skipped or snoozed, refills, and other changes you make.
  • Your records: personal info, medical history, health diary, appointments, emergency contacts, and any photos you add (pill photos, pharmacy labels, documents).
  • Your settings and the crash log: if the app ever crashes, technical details are kept on the phone so you can include them in a problem report.
  • All of it is kept in the app's private storage. The app is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so the system does not copy it to a cloud. Backups happen only when you export one yourself, to a file you choose.

Reading a pharmacy label

If you take or choose a photo of a pharmacy label, Pulse Meds reads the text on your phone with a built-in text reader. The photo and the text never leave the phone. The text is only shown to you to check; the app never fills in a dose by itself.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Notifications: to show your dose and appointment reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so a reminder rings at the time you set, not minutes later. If you turn this off, reminders still come, but Android may delay them.
  • Full-screen reminders: so a due dose can show over the lock screen like an alarm clock.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
  • Vibration and keep awake: to vibrate for a reminder and to keep the screen on while a reminder or the Desk Display is showing.

Pulse Meds does not ask for the camera, your location, your contacts, your phone calls or your files. Taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app, and saving or opening a backup uses Android's own file picker.

Calls, email and Google Play

When you tap to call an emergency contact, Pulse Meds opens your phone app with the number filled in; it never places a call by itself. "Check for updates" opens Pulse Meds in the Google Play Store app. Those apps make any connection, under their own privacy policies.

Problem reports

"Report a Problem" prepares an email in your own email app, to support@pulselabsstudio.com, with what you wrote and, if you leave it on, the app version, your phone and Android version, the crash log and the types of your last 10 actions. It never includes medication names. You see the email before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use a report only to answer you and fix the problem.

Children

Pulse Meds is made for adults. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored from your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them. Pulse Labs holds no copy of anything, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
