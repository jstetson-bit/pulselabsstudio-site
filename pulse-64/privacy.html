Pulse 64: Privacy Policy

Pulse Labs Studio · Effective November 21, 2026 · Applies to Pulse 64 for Android

Short version: Pulse 64 collects nothing and sends nothing. It does not even have permission to use the internet. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Pulse Labs never receives, stores, sells or shares any data about you.

What Pulse 64 reads, and where it stays

Pulse 64 shows how your phone is doing: battery level, temperature, voltage and charge rate; CPU speed and load; memory and storage; how fast data is moving; uptime; and your phone's model, chip and Android version. It reads these from Android on your phone and shows them on your phone. None of it leaves the phone.

  • Saved on the phone only: your settings, the panels you build in Panel Studio, your hologram colors and your benchmark scores (with the initials you type, if you type any).
  • Android backup is turned off for Pulse 64, so none of this is copied to any cloud. Uninstalling Pulse 64 removes all of it.

Problem reports

If you choose Report a problem, Pulse 64 fills in an email for you to send from your own email app: the app version, your phone model, Android version, screen size, a few settings, and the app's crash log if there is one. You see all of it before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use a report only to fix the problem, and you can ask us to delete it.

Permissions, and what each one is for

  • Display over other apps and run in the background: only for the optional face panel on lock, which shows your live readings over the lock screen when the screen turns off. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, and a notification shows the whole time it is on.
  • Notifications: that "Face panel is on" notification.
  • Start at boot: puts the face panel back after a restart, only if you had it on.

Pulse 64 has no internet permission, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, phone calls or text messages.

Children

Pulse 64 collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
